# Expense policy

## Submitting an expense
Submit business expenses in the expense portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Attach an itemised receipt, the business purpose, the cost centre, and the project code. Your direct manager approves expenses before Finance reimbursement.

## Travel and accommodation
Book travel through the approved travel portal where it is available. Economy travel is the default for flights under six hours. Hotel costs must be reasonable for the destination and travel dates.

## Non-reimbursable costs
Personal expenses, traffic fines, and alcohol without an approved client event are not reimbursable. Ask Finance before committing to an unusual or high-value cost.
