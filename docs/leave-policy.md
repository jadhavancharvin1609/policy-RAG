# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Submit requests in the HR portal at least five working days before the first day of leave whenever possible. The manager must approve the request before travel is booked.

## Sick leave
If you are unwell, notify your manager before your normal start time. A medical certificate is required only when an absence lasts more than three consecutive working days.

## Carry-over
Up to five unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Use carried-over leave by 31 March or it expires.
